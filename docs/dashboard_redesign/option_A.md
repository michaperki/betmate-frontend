⭐️ New Home Dashboard Concept (High-Level UI Blueprint)

A modern betting dashboard should do three things instantly:

show where you stand,

show what needs your attention,

get you into a match fast.

Your current screen tries to do that, but visually it’s scattered. The new design will be simple, punchy, and vertically structured for clarity.

✅ SECTION 1 — Header Snap-Summary (Hero Block)

A compact horizontal card at the top with your entire status in one glance:

[ Username + small avatar ]

Balance (big, bold)

Win rate

Total wagers

Active matches

Design notes:

One clean row, four metrics, equal-sized tiles

Dark background, soft neon outlines matching your brand

Eliminate the giant “Welcome back” text (too much vertical noise)

✅ SECTION 2 — Quick Action Bar

Right below the hero block:

Join Featured Match | View Active Bets | View Betting History

3 equal-width buttons → high-contrast glowing edges
This replaces scattered links and anchors attention.

✅ SECTION 3 — Featured Match (Full-Width Card)

This becomes THE centerpiece of the dashboard.

A full-width pill-shaped card with:

Player 1 (name + rating + piece icon)

VS block in the center

Player 2

Sub-badge: High Stakes / Low Stakes / Hot Match

Two actions:
Join Game (primary)
View Match (secondary, ghost)

Design notes:

Add a gentle gradient “glow” under the two piece icons

Replace the old “Featured Match” header with a clean emoji + title aligned left

Increase spacing so it feels like a live event card, not a settings panel

✅ SECTION 4 — Your Stats Blocks (Refined)

Three stacked tiles:

Wager Analytics – sparkline chart, showing last 10 bets
Recent Activity – last 3 moves you bet on
Current Streak – win/loss streak badge (gamifies retention)

Keep these tight — no big headers.

✅ SECTION 5 — Leaderboard (Condensed & Clean)

Small but premium:

Top 5 players only

Icons for 1st/2nd/3rd

Right-aligned tokens, but subtle, not bright red

Dropdown for All-Time / Monthly / Weekly

Add small profile avatars or color markers

Right now your leaderboard feels like an accounting table — let’s make it feel like a competition.

🎨 Visual Style Upgrades (Global)

Here’s the biggest improvement:
Switch from heavy neon to subtle glow accents.

Keep dark theme

Use thin neon outlines instead of thick neon fills

Slight glassy panels (2–5% white overlay)

Vertical spacing increased by 20% overall

Typography: slightly thinner, more modern, less bold spam

Result → premium esports analytics, not “casino UI.”
